## Final prompt

Create one clean 2x2 sprite-pose sheet for a Windows desktop pet, using the supplied images as strict identity and style references. This must be exactly the SAME character in every cell, not four reinterpretations.

Character invariants: chibi super-deformed girl with a very large head and tiny short body; identical round face and eye placement; dark navy-to-light-blue gradient long hair; one curved ahoge; white frilled maid headpiece; blue side bow on her right side; whale-fin/wing hair ornaments; navy-and-white maid dress; white apron with the exact blue whale emblem centered; gold ocean trim on skirt; short white socks and navy round shoes; blue whale tail behind her; large blue gradient eyes. Preserve all accessories, colors, line weight, proportions, hairstyle silhouette, outfit paneling and whale identity from the references. Do not redesign or simplify her.

Canvas: square image, strict 2x2 equal grid, one fully visible isolated character centered in each cell, same scale and baseline logic, generous padding, flat pure #00FF00 chroma-key background, no dividers, no labels, no captions, no shadow, no extra symbols, no watermark, no motion blur.

Pose order, left-to-right then top-to-bottom:

1. RELEASED / AIRBORNE: front three-quarter view, full body just let go, arms slightly lifted, legs hanging, surprised but cute, body clearly off the ground.
2. FALLING: same view and identity, hair and skirt lifted slightly by downward motion, compact body rotated only a little, worried expression, full body visible.
3. LANDING IMPACT: front three-quarter view, feet just contacting the ground, knees and tiny body compressed in a readable squash, arms balancing, hair and skirt settling outward, startled face; this is a brief impact key pose, not sitting and not injured.
4. RECOVERY: same character straightening back up after landing, one hand on head, slightly dizzy but standing, outfit and apron whale emblem fully intact.

The sequence should read as coherent animation key poses with a stable character volume and center of mass. High-quality polished 2D chibi game sprite art, crisp dark outlines, soft cel shading, transparent-ready edges against the green background.
